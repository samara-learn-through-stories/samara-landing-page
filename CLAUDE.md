# CLAUDE.md

## SEO / GEO Strategy

- Ensure pages load without JavaScript where possible - most AI crawlers cannot execute JS.
- Always keep static HTML content inside `<div id="root">` in `index.html` in sync with the landing page content — this is what crawlers see.
- Every page must have a unique `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and Open Graph tags.
- H1 and H2 tags must contain target keywords (apprendre l'arabe, lire en arabe, histoires en arabe, vocabulaire arabe, flashcard arabe).
- Maintain `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` in `/public`.
- Include JSON-LD structured data for rich results.
